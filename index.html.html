Pakistan Is More Than a Destination

Explore Pakistan is a travel-guide concept built around the country's landscapes, regional identities, heritage and everyday hospitality. Our goal is to help travellers prepare thoughtfully—not to promise that every route is always open or risk-free.

Good travel planning means checking current conditions, allowing extra time, supporting local businesses and listening to people who know the area.

Travel smart, travel respectfully

Important Travel Tips & Restrictions

Rules and conditions differ by location and can change. Use this as a preparation checklist, not as a substitute for official guidance.

✓ Before You Go

  • Carry identification: Keep valid ID; international visitors should carry passport/visa documents as appropriate.
  • Check weather and roads: Landslides, snow, floods and closures may affect mountain routes.
  • Pack essentials: Bring required medicines, water, suitable layers and a charged phone/power bank.
  • Respect local customs: Dress appropriately, especially at religious sites; remove shoes where requested.
  • Protect nature: Take rubbish back with you and do not disturb wildlife or damage heritage sites.
  • Use local guidance: Ask trusted local authorities or registered operators about route conditions.

! Restrictions to Check

  • Restricted and border areas: Some places may require special permission or may be closed to visitors.
  • Permits and registration: Requirements can vary by trek, protected area, route and nationality.
  • Photography: Never photograph military, security or other restricted installations. Ask permission before photographing people.
  • Religious and heritage sites: Follow posted rules on clothing, entry, footwear and photography.
  • Road and seasonal access: Some high-altitude routes are inaccessible or unsafe in winter or after severe weather.
  • Security advice: Avoid areas subject to official travel warnings; follow instructions from local authorities.
Important: We do not claim that a destination is currently open, permit-free or safe. Before booking, verify the latest advisories, permits, weather, road status and entry rules with relevant official authorities and your accommodation or licensed local operator.
